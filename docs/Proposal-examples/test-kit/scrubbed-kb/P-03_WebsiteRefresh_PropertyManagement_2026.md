# P-03 — Creative & Digital Solutions Proposal
**Client:** Client C (property management company)
**Date:** July 2026 · Valid 30 days from cover date
**Prepared by:** Javier Napoles

## Preferred New-Client Investment: $2,000
Website visual refresh + professional photography + tri-fold brochure design, offered as a focused first engagement to begin a long-term creative partnership.

## Project Overview
Client already had a functional digital foundation; opportunity was to improve visual presentation online and in prospect conversations, and establish a dependable ongoing creative resource. Replaced generic/outdated imagery, corrected third-party images on Google Business Profile, provided a professional brochure for business development.

## Service One — Website Visual Refresh + Professional Photography — $1,500
**Website refresh:** visual review, replace outdated imagery, minor layout/spacing refinements, typography/consistency improvements, image optimization, mobile review.
**Photography:** one on-site session up to 2 hrs, one location, office/lobby/workspace/environmental images, ~20 final edited photos, high-res + web-optimized exports, commercial use license.
**Google Business Profile:** selection of approved photos, correctly sized/named files, cover + exterior image replacement, one-time upload.
**Deliverables:** updated website visuals, web-optimized image library, high-res marketing image library, updated GBP photo set, recommendations for future improvements, up to 2 revision rounds.
**Scope boundaries (excluded):** full website redesign, new pages, copywriting, payment/application changes, integration development, advanced retouching, multiple locations, additional photo sessions, ongoing GBP management/review responses/monthly posts.

## Service Two — Tri-Fold Marketing Brochure — $500
Transform existing flyer/content/brand colors into a polished six-panel brochure. Includes: six-panel layout, current brand identity application, content hierarchy/readability, professional typography, print-ready PDF, digital PDF, editable source file, up to 2 revision rounds.
**Client-provided materials:** final approved copy, logo files, brand colors/assets, existing flyer, printer specs (if available).
**Not included:** printing costs, extensive copywriting, translation, custom illustration, additional formats, revisions beyond 2 rounds.

## Optional Ongoing Services (Section 05 — separate from the $2,000 engagement)
**Website Care & Support — $100/month:** 1 scheduled review, link/form/application testing, desktop+mobile checks, GBP photo/info check, up to 60 min minor content/image updates, priority 1-business-day response.
**Website Care, Support & SEO Essentials — $150/month:** everything above plus monthly Search Console review, indexing/basic technical SEO check, broken link/metadata review, 1 minor on-page SEO improvement/month, GBP info check, brief monthly summary, up to 90 min total work/month.
**Not included in either plan:** new pages/redesign/new photography/integrations, hosting/domain/licenses/security monitoring/backups, content writing/landing pages/link building/review management/monthly posts, advanced SEO/multi-page optimization. Additional work at $85/hr with written approval.
**Terms:** billed monthly in advance, month-to-month, 30-day cancellation notice, no rollover, rate locked 12 months.

## Optional Future Service — PDF Automation Feasibility Review — $200 (up to 4 hrs)
Review of forms/workflow, small proof-of-concept with synced fillable fields, test whether Adobe Acrobat native tools suffice, written summary of findings/limitations/next steps. If feasible, a separate implementation proposal follows with a **planning range of $1,000–$1,200** (not a final quote); the $200 fee credits toward approved implementation. Advanced automation (conditional logic) priced separately. Excludes production-ready system, final deployment, advanced JavaScript automation.

## Project Terms (general, reusable)
- Proposal valid 30 days from cover date.
- Two revision rounds included per service in the initial engagement.
- Requests outside defined scope quoted before work begins.
- Final editable files/full-res assets released after final payment.
- Third-party costs (printing, hosting, stock, plugins, licensing) not included unless stated in writing.
- Optional services require separate written approval before starting.
