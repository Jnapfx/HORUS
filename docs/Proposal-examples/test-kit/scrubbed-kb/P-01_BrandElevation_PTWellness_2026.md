# P-01 — Brand Elevation & Visual Alignment Proposal
**Client:** Client A (physical therapy & wellness practice — PT, Pilates, personal training under one roof)
**Date:** February 2026
**Prepared by:** Javier Napoles

## Executive Overview
Client A is positioned at the intersection of advanced clinical expertise and refined wellness integration (one-on-one, hour-long sessions across PT, Pilates, and personal training). The visual identity needed to communicate: upscale positioning, advanced/next-level care, clinical depth, integrated wellness, and a boutique, personalized experience. Goal: elevate external presentation to match the sophistication of the care delivered internally.

## Project Objectives
- Align visual identity with premium clinical positioning
- Create cohesion across service lines (PT, Pilates, Personal Training)
- Standardize outward-facing materials
- Strengthen authority with patients and referral partners
- Build infrastructure for future website and SEO expansion

## Timeline
- Week 1–2: Brand positioning clarification + logo concept development
- Week 3: Concept presentation + direction confirmation
- Week 4–5: Logo refinement + development of selected templates
- Week 6: Finalization + organized asset delivery
(Timeline may adjust based on feedback rounds and internal review.)

## Pricing Options

**Option 1 — Core Brand & Digital Communication Package — $2,500**
Core Brand Identity: logo refinement (2 strategic directions), final logo suite (horizontal/stacked/icon-only/B&W), refined color palette, typography system, mini brand guide (condensed).
Email Communication System: standardized email signature system, newsletter header template, welcome email template, general branded email template.

**Option 2 — Print & Marketing Visibility Package — $3,800**
Everything in Option 1, plus:
Print & Professional Collateral: brochure template (print-ready + editable), referral sheet template, business card design, branded presentation template, workshop/event flyer template.
Social & External Visibility: 4–6 social media templates, highlight cover system, visual grid structure guidance, social cohesion framework.

**Upgrade policy:** Option 1 → Option 2 upgrade within 90 days credits the initial investment toward the full package. After 90 days, upgrades are re-scoped and re-priced.

## Payment Terms
50% to initiate / 50% upon final delivery.
