# P-04 — Marketing & Design Proposal
**Client:** Client D (physical therapy clinic — returning client, previous website had lapsed)
**Date:** October 2025
**Prepared by:** Javier Napoles

## Overview
Comprehensive plan to modernize the clinic's brand and strengthen online + physical presence: website rebuild under a new domain, photo session, updated print materials, and redesign of exterior signage/window vinyls for a consistent identity across platforms.

## Background & Rationale
Original website (built by Javier previously) was lost when domain/hosting lapsed from lack of ongoing maintenance — rationale for including an ongoing management add-on this time.

## Goals
Modern professional appearance · secure/stable presence under new domain · visual consistency across digital/print/signage · support patient engagement/referrals · quality results within a focused budget and short timeline.

## Scope of Work
**A. Website Rebuild:** full visual redesign (new layout/colors/imagery), SEO optimization, mobile responsiveness, hosting/domain setup.
**B. Mini Photo Session:** half-day session — team portraits, treatment scenes, clinic environment; light editing for web/marketing.
**C. Business Cards & One-Page Brochure:** clean modern design aligned to new brand, QR code to website, print-ready files + production coordination.
**D. Exterior Signage & Window Vinyls (optional):** redesign of exterior banner + perforated (one-way vision) window vinyl graphics. Design-only estimate $300–$450; printing/installation via local vendor.

## Timeline
Content Planning (kickoff, finalize materials) — 1 week · Design & Photo Session — 1 week · Implementation (website/print finalization) — 1–2 weeks.

## Estimated Investment
| Service | Deliverables | Estimated Cost |

| Website Rebuild | Visual updates, hosting setup, mobile optimization, SEO | $950–$1,400 |

## Optional Add-On: Web Management & Maintenance
Domain/hosting management, regular backups/monitoring, minor text/image updates, priority technical support, quarterly SEO checks. **Flat fee: $150 every 3 months.**

## Complimentary Add-On: Local Outreach & Marketing Distribution
Compile a verified contact list of 50–75 local referral-relevant providers within a 25-mile radius; prepare marketing packets (business card + brochure + intro letter). Included at no charge as a value-add to support the brand launch.

## Next Steps
Confirm scope and sign · purchase/set up new domain + hosting · schedule photo session, provide materials · approve final designs for print/signage/website launch.
